WHY SWAY

You shouldn't have to pause your flow just to watch a ten-second video.

Most desktop audio controls force you to constantly switch between windows, ride volume sliders, and mute tabs. Sway operates on a simple belief: your operating system should intelligently balance your music and media without you ever having to think about it.

01

AUTONOMY

Zero manual switching. When you start playing a video or meeting audio, your music automatically steps aside. When audio finishes, it smoothly steps back.

02

RESTRAINT

Built to be invisible. Sway lives silently in the background with zero popups, zero network telemetry, and 0.0% CPU overhead when idle.

03

INTENTION

Selective precision. Only your chosen background music ducks. Voice chats, critical alerts, and games remain untouched at full volume.

SYSTEM INTEGRATION

Built with surgical precision.

Designed as a high-performance Windows desktop utility without audio virtualization drivers.

01 Process Isolation

Targeted Application Routing

Sway isolates background media applications like Spotify, Apple Music, VLC, and Tidal. WhatsApp, Discord voice channels, Steam games, and system alerts stay at 100% volume.

03 Linear Mathematics

Linear Fade Curves

Volume transitions are interpolated across configurable millisecond durations (default: 1000ms down, 1500ms restore delay, 1000ms up) to eliminate harsh volume snaps.
