A TACTILE AUDIO UTILITY FOR WINDOWS

Music dips. Video plays.
Music returns.

Sway is a lightweight background companion that detects when you play video audio in your browser and automatically lowers your music volume to 40%. When the video stops, your music smoothly steps right back.

Download Sway for Windows Windows 10 & 11 • 64-bit • 1.2 MB • Free & Open Source

Sway is a simple desktop app made for people who like listening to music while watching videos. Instead of constantly reaching for the volume controls, Sway handles it automatically. When you start playing a video, Sway lowers the volume of your music app to around 40–50%, so you can actually hear the video without completely losing your music. As soon as you pause the video, your music goes back to 100% volume. When the video starts again, the music lowers again. That’s basically it — no complicated setup, no constantly switching between apps, and no manually adjusting volume every time. Sway is meant to stay quietly in the background and just make multitasking with audio feel smoother. You can also customize how much the music volume drops, so it works the way you prefer.
